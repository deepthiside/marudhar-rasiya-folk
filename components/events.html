Royal Folk Performances

Experience the vibrant rhythms and legendary dance forms of Rajasthan.

World Famous

Kalbeliya Dance

The legendary snake charmer dance, recognized by UNESCO for its fluid, rhythmic movements.

Ghoomar Dance

The royal dance of Rajasthan, known for its graceful pirouettes and colorful swirling skirts.

Spectacular

Fire Dance

An awe-inspiring Jasnathi performance featuring brave dancers moving over live embers.

Chari Dance

A mesmerizing pot dance where performers balance lit brass pots with breathtaking skill.

Geramin Bhavai

A thrilling balancing act featuring multiple earthen pots and dancing on sharp edges.

Tera Tali Dance

A ritualistic dance performed with thirteen cymbals tied to the body, creating divine music.

Chang Dhamal

The energetic heartbeat of Shekhawati, performed with large drums during Holi festivities.

Binjara Dance

A vibrant nomadic dance celebrating the traveling spirit and folklore of the Marwar tribes.

Ladies Bhavai Nirtye

A display of incredible female strength and balance through traditional pot-balancing arts.
