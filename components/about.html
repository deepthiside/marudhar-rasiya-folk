About Us

Welcome to Marudhar Rasiya Folk

We carry the rich, vibrant heritage of Rajasthan straight to your celebrations. With over a decade of excellence in traditional folk arts, we bring the soul of the desert to life.

We specialize in mesmerizing performances of Kalbeliya, Ghoomar, Chari Dance, and authentic Langa Manganiyar music. Every beat of our Dhol and every twirl of our dancers tells a story of royalty, valor, and celebration, making your events truly unforgettable.
