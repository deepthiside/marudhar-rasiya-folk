About Us

Welcome to Marudhar Rasiya Folk

We carry the rich, vibrant heritage of Rajasthan straight to your celebrations. With over a decade of excellence in traditional folk arts, we bring the soul of the desert to life.

We specialize in mesmerizing performances of Kalbeliya, Ghoomar, Chari Dance, and authentic Langa Manganiyar music. Every beat of our Dhol and every twirl of our dancers tells a story of royalty, valor, and celebration, making your events truly unforgettable.

Our Global Footprint

"From the heart of Rajasthan to the global stage, Marudhar Rasiya is a trusted name in cultural entertainment. We have successfully executed spectacular events across major Indian cities, including New Delhi, Gurgaon, Chandigarh, Jaipur, Udaipur, Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Kolkata. Our global footprint includes highly acclaimed performances in Dubai, Qatar, London, New York, and Sydney. With a deep passion for authenticity, our traditional dance troupes and folk singing groups continue to deliver remarkable success at luxury weddings, private gatherings, and corporate events worldwide."
